Privacy policy

How we handle your enquiry details.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

  • Who we are. Enquiries about the independent house for sale in Thaltej, Ahmedabad are handled by Anu Srivastav, Property Navigators LLP, Thaltej, the agent appointed by the property owner.
  • What we collect. What you enter in the enquiry form on this website or in a Facebook or Instagram form: your name, mobile number, email if you give it, buyer type, budget, buying timeline and whether you want a site visit. We also record which page or ad you came from.
  • Why. Only to reply to your enquiry about this property, answer your questions and arrange a site visit.
  • How we contact you. By call, SMS or WhatsApp, and only if you ticked the consent box. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Who sees it. The agent, and the property owner, who receives updates on enquiries and offers. We do not sell or rent your details, and we do not share them with other sellers or brokers.
  • Where it is kept. In a private Google Sheet that only the agent and the owner can open. Leads from Facebook or Instagram forms are also held by Meta under its own policy.
  • How long. Until the property is sold, and then deleted within 90 days of the sale closing.
  • Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Contact and grievances. Anu Srivastav, Property Navigators LLP, Thaltej: +91 72260 61116. We will respond within 7 days.
  • Website. This website may use the Meta Pixel to measure which ads bring enquiries. It does not show you third-party ads.
